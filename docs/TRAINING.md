# Training scripts

## Observers — 30 minutes

1. Safety: this is not consent, not a prescription, not a diagnosis (2 min).
2. Install PWA (browser → Add to Home Screen) and unlock with PIN (3 min).
3. Find **Greeting**, **Pain**, **I need a Turkish nurse** and play audio (10 min).
4. Favourites and search in PT / TR / EN (5 min).
5. When **not** to use draft speech; always Confirm with staff (5 min).
6. Night shift: dark mode (2 min).
7. Questions (3 min).

## Ward nurses / preceptors — 15 minutes

1. Observers are **observers**; licensed staff remain accountable (3 min).
2. What the cards look like; they may read the Turkish line (5 min).
3. Limits: allergies, medicines, consent, emergencies — call the team, do not trust draft speech (5 min).
4. How to freeze a category / who to call (2 min).
