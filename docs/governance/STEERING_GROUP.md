# Steering group — Ankara University hospital translation platform

**Programme:** Ankara University + TİKA + Mozambique Ministry of Health (maternal and child health; observer placements at Ankara University Hospitals).  
**Product:** Controlled clinical communication aid (Portuguese–Turkish–English). Not a certified medical device and not an official interpreter.

## Mandate

Approve phrase packs, KVKK/IT constraints, pilot wards, freeze rules after safety events, and go/no-go for speech (phase 2) and documents (phase 3).

## Roles

| Seat | Typical holder | Decision rights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chair | Faculty of Nursing coordinator (or hospital nursing directorate delegate) | Agenda, sign-off on phrase pack versions |
| Clinical lead | Obstetric or neonatal consultant on the pilot unit | Clinical accuracy of maternity/neonatal phrases |
| Nursing preceptorship | Ward nurse manager / preceptor | Ward workflow, emergency wording, unit landmarks |
| Mozambique nurse representative | Observer cohort lead | Portuguese wording, Mozambique practice notes |
| Project / TİKA liaison | Programme coordinator | Cohort dates, invite list, training windows |
| Hospital IT / KVKK | Hospital information security or DPO delegate | Hosting, PIN access, no-PHI rule, logging |

Quorum: chair + clinical lead + nursing preceptorship + one of (Mozambique representative or TİKA liaison). IT must be present for any change that stores data or uses a network translation API.

## Phrase-review process (summary)

Full procedure: [PHRASE_REVIEW.md](./PHRASE_REVIEW.md).

1. Draft from two shadowed shifts on the pilot unit.  
2. Dual review: bilingual clinician (PT–TR or PT–EN plus TR native) and a Turkish preceptor.  
3. Version tag (`pack-YYYY.MM.DD`). Observers refresh on Wi‑Fi.  
4. Any allergy/medication misunderstanding attributed to the app **freezes** that category until rewritten and re-signed.

## Meeting cadence (pilot)

- Kick-off (week 0): PIN, ward poster, training.  
- Mid-pilot (week 2): phrase freeze list, search failures.  
- Close (week 4): Likert results, go/no-go for speech.

## Document control

Phrase JSON in the app is the operational pack. Signed review sheets stay with the Faculty of Nursing coordinator (paper or university drive). Do not put patient names in review notes.
