# Four-week ward pilot protocol

**Site (suggested):** one unit already in the Mozambique programme — obstetrics or neonatology, Ankara University Hospitals.  
**People:** 10–20 observers + 2–3 Turkish preceptors.

## Ethics / quality (short)

This is a **communication aid**, not a research study on patients. No patient identifiers are collected. If the faculty later wants a study, submit a separate ethics file.

**Patient poster** (print from the app, Training → Poster): staff may use a translation phone; the patient may always ask for a Turkish nurse.

## Week 0

- Steering group signs [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
- Content workshop (2 days) against real shadowing; update the pack.
- Change cohort PIN. Train observers (30 min) and ward nurses (15 min) using in-app Training.

## Weeks 1–4

- Phrasebook is default. Draft speech only after preceptors agree the phrasebook is trusted (week 2+).
- Daily: freeze `allergies_meds` if any medicine/allergy confusion is reported.
- Success: greeting + pain + “I need a nurse” without an interpreter in most **routine** moments; preceptors willing to continue.

## Evaluation (in-app Pilot)

After week 4 (and optionally week 2):

- Time to find a phrase (self-report bands).
- Missed communication incidents (self-report, no names).
- Observer confidence and preceptor confidence (1–5 Likert).
- List phrases to drop or rewrite.

Export results from the device only as anonymous JSON if IT allows.
